jlt-commons

A community-led home for Jolt libraries and tooling.

Not an official Jolt project. The language and the libraries its core team maintains live in jolt-lang.

Adoption

A Jolt project whose maintainer can no longer look after it, taken over so the community keeps a working version. We ask the current owner first, every time, and we prefer a transfer over a fork so the stars and the issue history come along.

Incubation

New community work. Ports of widely used Clojure libraries, and new libraries the core team does not want to own. The bar is a real gap and someone willing to maintain it, which is deliberately low while Jolt is this young.

Getting involved

You do not need to be a member. Open an issue or a pull request on any repository here.

  • Propose a project, or volunteer to maintain one
  • Governance and how to propose, both short
  • Talk to us on #jolt on the Clojurians Slack
